Mike Black

Founder and CEO

Mike founded Raven Guard after a career spent as an enterprise architect, security engineer and technology leader at Fortune 1 scale, where he watched good teams lose months to the wrong consultants: slide decks instead of pull requests, juniors billed as seniors, and tools nobody could run after the engagement ended.

Raven Guard is the answer to that. Senior engineers who ship, security people who close findings in code, AI built with evaluation and a rollback plan, and leaders who set up a cadence and then hand it over. Every engagement comes with the tooling to do the work and the people who know how to use it.

The mission is simple: leave every client stronger than we found them, with systems they own, numbers they trust, and a team that no longer needs us.
