Three ways to work with us.

Pick the shape that matches the problem. All three come with a named lead, a weekly written update, and an end date we agree up front.

Embedded team

One to four of our engineers join your team on your tools and your cadence. Billed monthly, rolled off as your hires land.

Fits: a roadmap that is bigger than the team, or a skill gap you cannot hire for in time.

Fixed-scope project

A defined outcome, a fixed price, and a handover that includes the documentation and the people who will own it.

Fits: migrations, assessments, an evaluation harness, a first production agent.

Advisory and fractional leadership

A senior leader on a retainer, from a few hours a month to three days a week, with a successor plan from day one.

Fits: a team between leaders, a board that wants a second opinion, a founder who needs a CTO this quarter.

How the first month runs

Scope

We read the code, the backlog and the incident history before we write a proposal. You get a one-page plan and a price.

Embed

Our people get access, join standup and pick up real work. Tools are installed and pointed at your systems.

Deliver

First pull requests, first findings closed, first dashboard live. The weekly update starts and does not stop.

Review

We show what shipped, what we learned, and whether the scope still holds. You decide what month two looks like.
